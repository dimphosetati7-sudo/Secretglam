Our Policies

Our policies are here to ensure every appointment runs smoothly and provides the best experience for all clients.

Booking Policy

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment. Your booking is only confirmed once the deposit has been received.

Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

Please let us know at least 24 hours before your appointment if you need to cancel or reschedule.

If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice or do not attend your appointment, your deposit will be forfeited.

Late Arrival Policy

Please arrive on time for your appointment.

Clients who arrive more than 30 minutes late will be charged a R50 late fee. If there is not enough time to complete the service, the appointment may be rescheduled.

Payment Policy

We accept EFT payments only.

The remaining balance must be paid before the service begins.

Satisfaction Policy

Your satisfaction is important to us. If you are unhappy with your service, please contact us within 48 hours. We will do our best to find a suitable solution.

Hygiene Policy

All tools and equipment are cleaned and sanitised before every appointment. We use quality products to ensure the safety of every client.

Appointment Preparation

Please:

  • Arrive with clean lashes and no eye makeup.
  • Inform us of any allergies or eye sensitivities before your appointment.

Lash Aftercare

  • Avoid oil-based products around your eyes.
  • Do not rub or pull your lash extensions.
  • Clean your lashes regularly using a lash-safe cleanser.
  • Book a refill every 2 to 3 weeks to maintain a full look.
