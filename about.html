About Us

Our Story

Secret Glam by Dimpho was created from a love for beauty and confidence. My journey started in August 2024 when I began doing my own lashes. I discovered that having my lashes done made me feel more confident and beautiful.

In December 2024, I started doing lashes for other people. As my passion grew, I decided to improve my skills by studying professional lash techniques. In 2026, I became a certified lash technician.

Today, Secret Glam by Dimpho proudly serves clients in Pretoria and Polokwane, offering affordable and professional lash and brow services in a clean and welcoming environment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help every client feel confident and beautiful by providing high-quality and affordable lash and brow services.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted beauty brand in Pretoria and Polokwane, known for quality, professionalism and excellent customer service.
