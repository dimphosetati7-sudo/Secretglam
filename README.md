## 1. Project Overview

Secret Glam by Dimpho is a small beauty business based in Pretoria and Polokwane, South Africa. The business offers affordable lash and brow services to clients who want to look and feel more confident.

The business started from a passion for beauty and lashes. The founder, Dimpho, started doing her own lashes in August 2024. In December 2024, she started doing lashes for other people. In 2026, she completed professional lash training and became a certified lash technician.

The purpose of this website is to create a professional online presence for Secret Glam by Dimpho. The website makes it easier for clients to view the available services, prices, business policies and contact information without having to ask for all the information through WhatsApp or social media.

### Business Slogan

**"Confidence Starts with Beautiful Lashes."**

---

## 2. Target Audience

The target audience for Secret Glam by Dimpho is people between the ages of **16 and 60** who are interested in affordable beauty services.

The main target locations are:

- Pretoria
- Polokwane

The business aims to provide affordable services for clients with low to medium income levels.

The website is designed to be simple and easy to use so that clients can quickly find information about services, prices and bookings.

---

## 3. Business Goals and Objectives

### Goal 1: Increase Bookings

Make services and prices available online so that clients can easily understand what is offered and make booking decisions.

### Goal 2: Build Customer Trust

Build trust by providing information about the founder, the business story, hygiene practices and business policies.

### Goal 3: Make Enquiries Easier

Provide an enquiry and booking form where clients can provide their details, select a service and choose a preferred appointment date.

### Goal 4: Build a Professional Online Presence

Create a professional website that represents Secret Glam by Dimpho and gives clients an easy way to find important business information.

The website aims to:

The Home page introduces Secret Glam by Dimpho and includes:

- Business logo.
- Hero image.
- Business name.
- Business slogan.
- Short introduction.
- Featured services.
- Why Choose Us section.
- Book Now link.
